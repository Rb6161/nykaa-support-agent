# Warranty terms by category

Electronics such as hair dryers, trimmers and straighteners carry a manufacturer warranty of 6 to 12 months, as printed on the product page and invoice. Home appliances and decor with electrical parts have a 6-month warranty. Apparel, footwear and beauty products have no warranty, but manufacturing defects reported within the return window are covered by a replacement or refund. Warranty claims need the invoice and the product serial number. For any warranty case that these terms do not clearly cover, the assistant must not decide on its own and should escalate the case to a human support agent.
