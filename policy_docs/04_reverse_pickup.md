# Reverse-pickup eligibility

Reverse pickup is offered for eligible orders on serviceable pin codes when the return is raised inside the return window. The courier makes up to 3 pickup attempts over 3 working days. Pickup is not available for opened beauty products, items without original packaging, or pin codes outside the courier network. In those cases the customer can self-ship the product and the shipping cost is reimbursed after approval. A pickup request is closed automatically if the customer cannot be reached on all three attempts, and to re-initiate the pickup the customer must contact customer support, where an agent will raise a ticket only if the case is found genuine.
