# Order status meanings

Placed means the order is confirmed and is being packed, and it has not yet been handed to the courier. Shipped means the courier has the parcel and a tracking link is available. Delivered means the parcel reached the address, and the return window starts from that date. Returned means the item came back to the warehouse, and Refunded means the money has been sent back to the customer. A delayed shipment keeps its current status, and the customer can ask for a status check.
