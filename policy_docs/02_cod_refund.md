# Cash on Delivery refund timelines

Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders cannot go back to the original payment method, so they are paid by bank transfer. The customer must share a valid account number and IFSC code, or a UPI ID, in the refund request. Once the returned item passes quality check, the refund is released within 5 to 7 working days. If the bank details are wrong, the refund is held until the customer corrects them. Customers receive an SMS or email as soon as the bank transfer has been initiated.
