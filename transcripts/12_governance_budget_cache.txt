##### APPLICATION LAYER: risk classification #####
Risk level: Medium
This system is a customer-support assistant that answers policy questions and reads order status, which falls in the Medium tier (customer support tickets). It is not Low, because it touches customer order records and receives phone numbers and card last-4 digits, which are personal data. It is not High, because it handles no medical data, makes no hiring or credit decisions, cannot move money (it only quotes refund policy), and has read-only access to fabricated order data. Its answers are grounded in a fixed knowledge base, reviewed by a second agent team, and low-confidence cases are refused or escalated to a human.
