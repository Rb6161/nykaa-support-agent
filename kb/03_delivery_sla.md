# Delivery SLAs

Metro cities receive standard orders within 2 to 4 days after dispatch. Tier-2 and tier-3 towns and remote pin codes take 5 to 8 days. Express delivery, where available, reaches eligible pin codes within 24 to 48 hours. A shipment is considered delayed when it is more than 2 days past its promised delivery date, and the customer can then ask for a status check.
