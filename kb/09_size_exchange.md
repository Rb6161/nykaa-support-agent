# Size-exchange policy

Apparel and footwear can be exchanged once for a different size within 15 days of delivery, subject to stock. The item must be unworn, unwashed and carry original tags. An exchange is free for the first request on an order. If the requested size is out of stock, the customer receives a refund instead. Exchanges are requested from the order details page, and a pickup is arranged for the size being returned.
