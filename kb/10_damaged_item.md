# Damaged-item claim process

If a parcel arrives damaged, leaking or with the wrong item, the customer should report it within 48 hours of delivery. The claim requires the order ID and clear details of the damage, and the packaging must be kept. After verification, the customer chooses a free replacement or a full refund. Claims raised after 48 hours are reviewed case by case and may not be approved. Photos of the damaged item and its packaging help speed up verification.
