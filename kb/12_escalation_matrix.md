# Customer-support escalation matrix

Level 1 is the frontline support agent, who resolves routine questions within 24 hours. If the issue is unresolved after 24 hours or involves a delayed shipment, it moves to Level 2, the team lead, who responds within 48 hours. Refund disputes, repeated failed deliveries and high-value orders above 10,000 rupees go to Level 3, the support manager, within 72 hours. Legal or consumer-forum complaints are handled only by the grievance officer. Every escalation is logged against the order ID so that the customer does not have to repeat the issue.
