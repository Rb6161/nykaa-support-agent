# Order-cancellation policy

An order can be cancelled free of charge while its status is Placed, which means it has not been handed to the courier. Once the order is Shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, and the customer should refuse delivery or raise a return after receiving it. Prepaid cancellations are refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Cancelling after a refusal at the doorstep may delay the refund until the parcel is back at the warehouse.
